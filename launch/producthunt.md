## First comment

Hi, I'm Josh, I built Hikko.

I wanted a place to drop half-formed ideas without the pressure of a full pitch deck or a "let's schedule a call." Hikko is a jar you throw ideas into. Post one, vote on others, comment, and every idea automatically grows a build spec and a step-by-step plan, written server-side so you don't have to do the busywork of turning a one-liner into something buildable. Every morning at 9am a fresh idea shows up on its own, seeded by the same engine, so the feed never goes stale even on a quiet day.

It runs as a PWA on the web and as native apps on iOS, macOS, and watchOS, with Face ID or Touch ID sign-in on iOS. Filter by category, sort by Hot or New, cluster related ideas into Idea Bases, export a thread as markdown. No ads, no growth hacks, just a fast feed and a way to see if an idea has legs before you commit a weekend to it.

macOS and iOS are both live on the App Store, and the web works everywhere. Free, with Spark Pro at $1 once on the web for anyone who wants to tip in for extras. The iOS build stays silent on that, App Review wants IAP for that kind of thing.
